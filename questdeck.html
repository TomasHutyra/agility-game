How it works

1. Pick a mood

Bored, outside, with friends, weekend — 8 moods to choose from.

2. Flip 3 cards

Three quest cards appear face-down. Tap to reveal them one by one.

3. Go do it

Choose one quest, do it, mark it done. Earn XP and level up.

Key features

✓100+ real-world quests — solo, couples, friends
✓Works fully offline — no internet required
✓No account, no login, no tracking
✓XP system, levels, and progress history
✓Easy, medium, and hard difficulty
✓Android — Google Play (coming soon)
