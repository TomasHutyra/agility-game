How it works

1
Pick a mood

Bored, outside, with friends, weekend — 8 moods to match where you are right now. QuestDeck meets you there.

2
Flip 3 cards

Three quest cards appear face-down. Tap to reveal them one by one — the suspense is half the fun.

3
Go do it

Pick one quest, go do something real, mark it done. Earn XP and level up your adventurer rank.
